Privacy Policy for PureAuth

Last Updated: June 2026

PureAuth ("we", "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains how our browser extension handles user data. PureAuth is a fully offline Time-based One-Time Password (TOTP) authenticator.

1. Data Collection & Transmission

Zero Data Collection: PureAuth operates strictly locally. There are no backend servers, and the extension contains no network request logic. We do NOT collect, store, transmit, or share your Personally Identifiable Information (PII), browsing history, or TOTP secret keys.

2. Permissions and Usage

To provide offline authentication, we request the following permissions in manifest.json:

  • storage: Used solely to securely persist your account data and configurations in your local browser storage. This data never leaves your device.
  • clipboardWrite: Triggered only when you explicitly click a verification code to copy it to your clipboard for convenience.

3. Data Deletion

You have full control over your data. You can delete accounts individually inside the extension, or completely erase all data from chrome.storage.local by uninstalling PureAuth.

4. Contact

If you have questions regarding this privacy policy, please contact us at: tailiqoutlook@gmail.com

PureAuth 隐私政策

更新日期：2026年6月

PureAuth（"我们"）深知隐私的重要性。本插件是一款完全离线运行的双重身份验证（TOTP）工具。

1. 数据收集与传输

绝对零收集： PureAuth 不设立任何后端服务器，不包含任何网络请求。我们不会收集、存储、传输或共享您的任何个人身份信息（PII）、浏览历史或双重认证密钥。

2. 权限使用及目的

  • storage （本地存储权限）： 仅用于在您的本地浏览器中安全保存您的 TOTP 账号列表及密钥配置。数据绝不会离开您的设备。
  • clipboardWrite （剪贴板写入权限）： 仅当您在插件 UI 中点击验证码时触发，用于快速复制到剪贴板。

3. 数据擦除

您的所有账户数据完全由您自主控制。您可以在插件中手动删除记录，或者直接通过在浏览器中“卸载” PureAuth 插件来彻底擦除本地存储中的所有数据。
